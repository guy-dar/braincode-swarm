<|user|>What is the tank mate compatibility of an octopus?
<|assistant|>Octopuses are solitary by nature. Introducing an octopus leads to negative interactions such as aggression and cannibalism. Their dietary role is that of an opportunistic predator. A stimulating environment for solitary enrichment is recommended, and introducing another octopus is opposed and should be avoided.
