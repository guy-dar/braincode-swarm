<|user|>Should I get my octopus a friend to play with in its tank or will it eat it?
<|assistant|>Octopuses are solitary creatures and generally do not benefit from tank mates. In fact, adding another octopus to your tank could lead to aggressive behavior, cannibalism, or other negative interactions. Octopuses are known to be opportunistic predators, and will often eat any smaller animal or invertebrate in their environment, including other octopuses. Therefore, it is best to avoid introducing another octopus to your tank and focus on providing a stimulating and enriching environment for your octopus alone.
