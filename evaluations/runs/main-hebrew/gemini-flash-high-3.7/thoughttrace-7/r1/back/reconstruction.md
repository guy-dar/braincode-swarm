## Dinner Idea: Taco Night
It's fun, casual, customizable, and very easy to make for a group.

### Main Components
- **Protein option:** Seasoned chicken or beef
- **Vegetarian option:** Black bean and roasted vegetable tacos
- **Taco shells:** Soft tortillas and/or crispy shells
- **Toppings:** Shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, cheese, sour cream, guacamole, salsa, cilantro, lime wedges

### Easy Sides
- Mexican rice or cilantro lime rice
- Corn salad or a simple green salad
- Tortilla chips with salsa and guacamole

## Why It Works
- Most prep can be done in advance
- Guests can build their own meal
- The vegetarian option feels just as satisfying as the meat option
- It's casual and festive

## Simple Vegetarian Filling
Try this:
- 2 cans black beans, drained
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 zucchini or mushrooms, chopped
- Olive oil
- Taco seasoning or cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, salt

Roast or sauté the vegetables, stir in the beans and seasoning, and cook until everything is warm.

## Meat Filling Idea
For chicken or beef:
- Cook with olive oil and taco seasoning
- Add a little lime juice at the end for extra flavor

## If You Want Dessert
Keep it simple:
- Store-bought churros
- Brownies
- Ice cream with cinnamon

## Another Easy Dinner Option

### Baked Pasta Night
- **Main dish:** Baked ziti or lasagna
- **Vegetarian option:** Spinach and ricotta baked pasta or vegetable lasagna

This is also great because you can assemble it ahead of time and just bake before guests arrive.
