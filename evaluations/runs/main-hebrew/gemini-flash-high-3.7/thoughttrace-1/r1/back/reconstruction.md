Hello! Gladly, here is a complete system to stay organized. I'll structure this into **main categories**.

---

## 🗂️ 1. The Story Bible
*The central reference document*

- **General synopsis** (pitch in 2-3 lines)
- **Main themes** you want to explore
- **Tone and atmosphere** (dark, humorous, dramatic...)
- **Target audience**
- **Estimated length** (one-shot, multi-chapter...)

---

## 👥 2. Character Sheets

For each character *(canon or original)*:

- Name / Nickname
- Role in your story
- **How you reinterpret them** relative to canon
- Personality traits
- Motivations and goals
- Planned narrative arc
- Relationships with the other characters

---

## 🌍 3. Worldbuilding and Setting

- **Source universe** *(canon compliant? divergent?)*
- Main location(s)
- Time period / Timeline
- **Points of divergence** from canon *(if AU or divergent from the source)*
- Specific rules for your universe

---

## 📋 4. Narrative Structure

### 3-Level Outline:

| Level | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 🔴 **Macro** | Acts / Main parts |
| 🟡 **Meso** | Narrative arcs by character |
| 🟢 **Micro** | Chapter-by-chapter outline |

### For each chapter:
- Narrative goal
- Planned scenes
- Characters present
- Plot progression
- Cliffhanger / Hook

---

## 🔄 5. Plot Tracking

- **Main plot**
- **Subplots** *(romance, mystery, secondary conflict...)*
- Tracking table *(which plots are active in each chapter)*
- **Unresolved** narrative threads not to forget

---

## ✍️ 6. Practical Tools

| Tool | Use |
|-------|-------|
| 📓 **Notion / Obsidian** | Complete story bible and sheets |
| 📝 **Google Docs** | Writing chapters |
| 🗃️ **Trello / Notion** | Progress tracking |
| 🖊️ **Scrivener** | All-in-one for writing |
| 📌 **Paper notebook** | Quick notes and inspiration |

---

## ✅ 7. Writing Routine

- **Before writing** → Re-read the chapter sheet
- **During** → Note inconsistencies to fix
- **After** → Update the plot tracker
